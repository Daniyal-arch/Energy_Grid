# Data requests

Generated by `scripts/data_requests.py` from the registries (`request` fields in
`scripts/wave*_sources.yaml`, "Data request:" in `data/coverage.yaml` notes) and the
hand-written ones in the script. Ready for the owner to send; nothing is sent by a
session. Each request should also ask the licence question:

> May we redistribute the data, and show it in a commercial product, under an open licence (CC BY 4.0 or equivalent) with attribution?

20 requests to 20 publishers.

## CLMS

- **Wetlands (AT)** (data/coverage.yaml `wetlands.AT`)
  - Why: free for any use, but the download needs an EU Login account (sources behind a login are out of scope).
  - Request: CLMS, an anonymous download or COG endpoint.

## Copernicus Land

- **European Ground Motion Service** (scripts/wave9_sources.yaml `eu_egms`)
  - Why: download behind EU Login
  - Request: Copernicus Land, a public bulk URL for EGMS ortho products

## Destatis

- **Destatis regional table 43531 (industry energy use per district)** (scripts/wave9_sources.yaml `de_destatis_43531`)
  - Why: GENESIS (Regionalstatistik) tables are downloadable only with a registered account
  - Request: Destatis, an open download URL for table 43531-01-02-4 (dl-de/by-2-0)

## EFFIS

- **Fires (AT)** (data/coverage.yaml `fires.AT`)
  - Why: FIRMS needs FIRMS_MAP_KEY (free; not set); EFFIS burnt-area perimeters are given through EFFIS's data request form.
  - Request: EFFIS, burnt area perimeters for reuse under CC BY 4.0.

## Elia

## Eurostat (GISCO)

- **Administrative units: countries, NUTS 2024 and LAU outlines** (docs/owner-questions.md)
