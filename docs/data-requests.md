# Data requests

Generated by `scripts/data_requests.py` from the registries (`request` fields in
`scripts/wave*_sources.yaml`, "Data request:" in `data/coverage.yaml` notes) and the
hand-written ones in the script. Ready for the owner to send; nothing is sent by a
session. Each request should also ask the licence question:

> May we redistribute the data, and show it in a commercial product, under an open licence (CC BY 4.0 or equivalent) with attribution?

26 requests to 26 publishers.

- **Wetlands (AT)** (data/coverage.yaml `wetlands.AT`)
  - Why: free for any use, but the download needs an EU Login account (sources behind a login are out of scope).
  - Request: CLMS, an anonymous download or COG endpoint.
