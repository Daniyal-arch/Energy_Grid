# 22. Wave 7: the same datasets for every EU country, the UK and the US

Start after wave 6 (brief 21) is merged. Read CLAUDE.md, this file, and brief 17's token rules and
templates (they apply unchanged, including "Run to the end" and "Done means"). No AI attribution
anywhere: commits, PR title and PR text carry no "Generated by" or "Co-Authored-By" lines.

## Goal

Today several datasets exist for a few countries only (grid capacity for GB, NL, ES and wave 6's
six; every unit for Germany; 5-minute data for Denmark and GB; redispatch for Germany; EV chargers for
the US; ...). This wave makes each **dataset family** consistent across the **27 EU countries, the UK
and the US**: for every family and every country, either a dataset on the map, or a documented
"not published" with the place where it was looked for. No gap stays unknown.

## Step 1: the coverage registry (first commit)

- `data/coverage.yaml`: one entry per family × country (EU-27, GB, US; US families may be per state
  or per market where that is how data is published): `status` (have / built / not_published /
  blocked), `source`, `url`, `licence`, `dataset` (the app file or lake table), `checked` (date),
  `note`. Pre-fill it from what exists today (docs/DATA_SOURCES.md, wave 5 and 6 outputs).
- `scripts/coverage.py` renders `docs/COVERAGE.md` (a country × family table with ✅ ◐ — ⛔) and
  `frontend/public/data/eu/coverage.json`; a test checks every family × country cell is filled.
- A "What we have for <country>" card in the country panel (a compact list of the families with
  their source, or "not published"), and the same table in the country report.
- `not_published` needs evidence: the operator's page checked and the date. It counts as done.

## Step 2: probe, then build without stopping

`scripts/probe_wave7.py` (one Actions run, one step per family, ≤ 10 notices each) checks the
candidate sources below; then build in family order. Prefer **one pan-European source** over many
national ones whenever it exists (ENTSO-E, EAFO, AGSI, Eurostat); fill the gaps with national
sources. A country whose operator publishes nothing machine-readable is `not_published` (no PDF
scraping, no estimates).

## The families (in this order)

### 1. Room to connect (extends the "Grid capacity" layer, site analysis "Room to connect")

Have: GB (TEC), NL, ES (REE nodes); wave 6: FR, IT, BE, PT, IE, PL.
- **Index first:** ENTSO-E's overview of TSO hosting-capacity publications (2025) and the EU DSO
  Entity's list; use them to find each operator's page.
- **Transmission, per country:** DE (the four TSOs' published connection capacities), AT (APG), DK
  (Energinet capacity map), SE (Svenska kraftnät capacity map), FI (Fingrid connection capacity),
  EE (Elering), LV (AST), LT (Litgrid), CZ (ČEPS), SK (SEPS), HU (MAVIR), SI (ELES), HR (HOPS),
  RO (Transelectrica), BG (ESO), GR (IPTO/ADMIE), LU (Creos), CY, MT.
- **Distribution where it is the main constraint:** GB DNOs' embedded capacity registers (all six
  publish monthly), ES DSOs' monthly node capacities (i-DE, e-distribución, UFD under CNMC rules),
  DE (VNBdigital / the large DSOs), AT (DSOs' capacity per substation).
- **US:** every ISO's live queue (CAISO, ERCOT, MISO, SPP, ISO-NE have; NYISO: try its queue file
  again with a browser fetch (`browser-fetch.yml`); PJM waits for `PJM_API_KEY`), plus the LBNL
  queue for non-ISO utilities (have). Utility hosting-capacity maps only where published as data
  (CA: PG&E, SCE, SDG&E ICA; NY Joint Utilities).

### 2. Every generating unit (national registers; layer per country under "Power plants")

Have: DE (MaStR), US (EIA-860M, USWTDB, USPVDB), Europe ≥ 20 MW (powerplantmatching), GRW worldwide.
- GB: Renewable Energy Planning Database (REPD, every renewable project from 150 kW with planning
  status) and DUKES major power producers list.
- FR: ODRÉ "Registre national des installations de production et de stockage" (Etalab).
- DK: Energistyrelsen master data of wind turbines; ES: MITECO/RAIPRE registry; IT: Terna/GSE open
  data; NL, BE, AT, PL, SE (Vindbrukskollen), FI (Energy Authority register), IE, PT, CZ and the rest:
  their national registers where published as data.
- Countries with none: `not_published`, and the map keeps powerplantmatching + GRW there.

### 3. Live mix and carbon intensity at the operator's own resolution (country cards)

Have: GB (Elexon 5 min, NESO CO₂), DK (Energinet 5 min), all EU via ENTSO-E (15 min).
- Operators' own feeds where they add something ENTSO-E does not (finer time step, CO₂ per kWh,
  regional split): FR (RTE éCO2mix incl. CO₂), ES (REE incl. CO₂), IE (EirGrid dashboard incl. CO₂),
  BE (Elia), NL (TenneT/NED), PL (PSE), PT (REN), AT (APG), IT (Terna), NO, SE, FI (Fingrid: needs
  `FINGRID_API_KEY`), DE (SMARD).
- US: EIA-930 hourly CO₂ emissions per balancing authority (EIA API), so every US region card shows
  CO₂ per kWh like GB and DK.
- CO₂ per kWh is shown only where the operator publishes it (never computed from emission factors).

### 4. Redispatch, curtailment, congestion costs (country cards, agent catalogue)

Have: DE 2013–2020 (netztransparenz).
- ENTSO-E for every TSO: redispatching (A63), countertrading (A91), costs of congestion management
  (A92): daily in `eu-days.yml`, lake `entsoe/congestion_*` (mind the ENTSO-E rate rules of wave 5).
- GB: NESO balancing services costs including constraint costs (NESO data portal).
- US: curtailment where markets publish it (CAISO wind and solar curtailment; ERCOT if published).

### 5. Prices with the same coverage

- GB day-ahead and imbalance prices (Elexon market index and system prices; ENTSO-E has no GB
  after 2021), so the Prices views and the imbalance card include GB.
- US real-time hub prices next to day-ahead (gridstatus), for an imbalance-like view in the US.

### 6. What consumers pay

Have: EU (Eurostat), US (EIA).
- GB: Ofgem price cap (households) and DESNZ Quarterly Energy Prices (non-domestic), so GB shows
  in the retail colouring and country report.

### 7. Emitters per installation

Have: EU ETS (EU, NO, IS, LI), US GHGRP, Climate TRACE worldwide.
- GB: UK ETS installation compliance data (DESNZ / UK ETS registry publications) into the same
  "CO₂ per installation" layer.

### 8. Plants offline now

Have: EU (ENTSO-E unavailability).
- GB: Elexon REMIT unavailability messages, so "Plants offline now" covers GB.

### 9. EV charging

Have: US (NREL AFDC).
- EU + GB: the European Alternative Fuels Observatory (counts per country), national open
  registers for the points (DE BNetzA Ladesäulenregister, FR IRVE consolidated file, NL, GB national
  chargepoint data where open), and OpenStreetMap charging stations as the common map layer where a
  register is missing (labelled by source).

### 10. Gas, US side

Have: Europe (ENTSOG flows, AGSI storage, GIE ALSI LNG).
- US: EIA weekly natural gas storage by region, Henry Hub and regional spot prices (EIA API), EIA
  natural gas pipelines and LNG terminals (EIA shapefiles), so the gas views work for the US.

### 11. Reliability

Have: US EAGLE-I outages per county.
- EU + GB: SAIDI/SAIFI per country and year where published as data (CEER benchmarking data,
  national regulators); `not_published` otherwise.

## Out of scope

The backend and the agent, new countries outside EU-27, GB and US, keyed sources still waiting
(listed in the registry as `blocked` with the env var), redesigns of existing layers.
