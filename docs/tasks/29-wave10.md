# Brief 29: data wave 10 (hundreds of country, world and GB district series)

Asked on 2026-11-03: a new big wave of datasets, gathered first and then added, each one
working everywhere the app works: the map (layers, tiles or colourings), the backend (DuckDB,
`app.*` and lake tables), the LangGraph agent's tools (search_catalog, describe_table, run_sql
with `in_area`, area_stats, resolve_area) and the MCP server, the catalogue, levels.yaml,
coverage and evals.

## Gathering (`scripts/probe_wave10.py`, evidence in `data/wave10_gather.json`)

- **Eurostat**: the table of contents, filtered to energy (`nrg_`), environmental accounts
  (`env_ac_`, `env_air`, `env_epea`, `env_esst`, `env_wat`), the SDG energy, climate, water,
  city and industry tables, vehicles by fuel (`road_eqs`, `road_eqr`), rail and inland waterway
  equipment and infrastructure, circular economy (`cei_`) and the `ten`/`t2020` headline
  tables, minus every code an earlier wave or the catalogue already names and tables ending
  before 2022: 323 tables. Each table's newest period read as JSON-stat; one published series
  chosen per table for the map (a total when the table has one, else the code covering most
  countries); 241 publish it for 15 or more countries and become country colourings.
- **World Bank WDI**: every indicator of the topics Energy & Mining, Environment,
  Infrastructure and Climate change (225), each one's licence read from its series metadata
  (all CC BY 4.0) and the countries with a value in the newest year.
- **GB per local authority district**: DESNZ sub-national electricity, gas and total final
  energy consumption, renewable capacity, generation and sites, local greenhouse gas
  emissions; DfT public EV charging devices; ONS Census 2021 (Nomis) heating, accommodation,
  cars, tenure, bedrooms, travel to work, density, households. Hand-picked in
  `scripts/wave10_manual.yaml`, coloured on ONS December 2024 district shapes (`gb_lad24`).

## Building

`python scripts/probe_wave10.py registry` writes `scripts/wave10_sources.yaml` (571 sources,
484 colourings). The wave-9 machinery runs it with `WAVE=10`: lake dataset `wave10`, app files
`eu/w10/` and `eu/w10c/`, manifest `frontend/src/app/wave10.json`, catalogue
`agent/catalog/wave10.yaml`, levels rows, coverage families (`scripts/wave10_families.yaml`),
docs blocks and `docs/research/wave10-sources.md`. Builds run in Actions
(`.github/workflows/wave9.yml`, commit message `[w10: 1 2 ...]`).

Rules as ever: every value as published (a colouring is a filter on the published table,
never a sum or estimate); sources named on screen; CC BY 4.0, OGL v3 or public domain only.
